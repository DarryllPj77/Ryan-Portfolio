Lakbay: The Journey of Learning

Lakbay represents the journey of every student—from the first step into the school, through the continuous process of learning, interaction, and discovery, toward personal growth and graduation. The school is envisioned not merely as a place for academic instruction, but as a dynamic environment where every space contributes to the students’ educational experience. Through interconnected learning areas, flowing circulation, communal spaces, and opportunities for interaction with nature, the design encourages students to explore, collaborate, and develop their potential. The curved architectural form symbolizes the continuity of this journey, guiding students through different spaces and experiences while expressing movement, connection, and progression. By integrating functional planning, climate-responsive strategies, and welcoming outdoor spaces, LAKBAY transforms the school into a meaningful journey where the environment supports not only academic achievement but also social development, creativity, and personal growth.

Facade perspective

The dynamic, undulating facade translates the student experience into a physical architectural skin: the fluid, non-linear profile reflects an academic journey filled with moments of challenge, momentum, and growth. Each individual wave panel acts as a single student's trajectory, which, when repeated across the building elevation, weaves together to form a collective identity representing how diverse personal paths shape a shared educational community.
