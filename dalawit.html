Dalawit: Interlocked Sanctuaries

DALAWIT: Duality — Interlocked Sanctuaries is an architectural concept inspired by the principles of duality, interlock, and sanctuary, representing two individual homes connected through a unified architectural composition. The concept explores the balance between togetherness and individuality, allowing two households to coexist harmoniously while maintaining their own identity, privacy, and comfort. Through duality, the design expresses two distinct residential units within a shared architectural form, while interlock translates their connection into the arrangement of building masses and spaces. Sanctuary emphasizes the creation of a safe, peaceful, and private environment where each family can experience comfort and belonging. Ultimately, DALAWIT envisions a harmonious duplex where two homes are interconnected yet remain distinct, balancing architectural unity with personal independence and providing each household with its own private sanctuary.

Interior feature wall

The feature wall combines organic patterns with modern interior design strategies, creating a visually striking focal point that adds elegance, texture, and character to the living space. The decorative panels feature intricate leaf-inspired forms that introduce natural elements into the interior, while their metallic finish adds sophistication and visual depth. The use of vertical arrangements emphasizes the room's height and creates a sense of continuity. Complementing the feature wall, the neutral color palette, minimalist furniture, warm accent lighting, and large windows establish a balanced and contemporary atmosphere. This combination of natural inspiration and modern detailing creates an interior that is both aesthetically refined and welcoming.
